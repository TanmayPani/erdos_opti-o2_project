Know Before You Go

Hard Probes · Arrival Guide · June 19, 2025

Check-In Location
Building
Rothschild College
Address (drop-off only)
2401 West End Ave, Nashville, TN 37203
Check-In Window
June 19 – 21 · 7:00 AM – 10:00 PM

Please check in during staffed hours on June 19th, 20th, or 21st. Bring a valid government-issued ID to receive your room key and suite access card.

If you will not be arriving between 7:00 AM and 10:00 PM, please notify us via email in advance and call 615-582-9974 upon your arrival. This line is available 24/7 and is your point of contact for any needs related to the residential college.
Navigating & Unloading

Do not approach from West End Ave — that road is exit-only. Instead, turn onto 25th Ave, then left onto Kensington Place. Follow the Rothschild College signs to the 30-minute unloading zone.

If arriving by rideshare, you may be dropped on West End and walk along the exit road to the college.

30-minute limit in unloading spaces. Hazard lights must remain on; do not block traffic. Move your vehicle as soon as unloading is complete.
Parking Information

If you prepaid for parking at registration, you will receive your parking pass at check-in. You will park in the 25th & Highland Garage on floors 4–11.

Do not back into parking spaces. Your rear license plate must remain visible at all times.
Moving Carts & Keys

A limited number of moving carts are available on a first-come, first-served basis and require collateral at check-out. Please return them promptly for others to use.

Your room key and suite access card are issued at check-in and work only for your assigned suite.

Room assignments have been made based on requests submitted. Please check your room upon arrival — if you believe there has been an error, notify staff right away so we can resolve it promptly.
We look forward to welcoming you to Vanderbilt!
